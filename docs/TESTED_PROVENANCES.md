# Camcore Tested Provenances

Collection 05 maps geographic origins represented in the Camcore provenance catalogue.
The current catalogue contains 913 records with valid coordinates, representing 80 species
and 20 countries. Species are organized into Eucalypts, Corymbias, Pines and Other species.

Viridis colors distinguish provenance records within the selected species; they do not encode
climatic suitability or performance. Labels are automatically shown for small collections and
at detailed zoom levels to preserve map readability.
