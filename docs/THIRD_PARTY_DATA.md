# Third-party data and redistribution

## ANADEM

ANADEM v1 is a publicly available digital terrain model for South America. The primary publication states that the generated data are freely available for use through Google Earth Engine and MGRS downloads from the official project website.

- Citation: Laipelt et al. (2024), *Remote Sensing* 16, 2321.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/rs16132321
- Official project: https://hge-iph.github.io/anadem/
- Google Earth Engine asset: `projects/et-brasil/assets/anadem/v1`

Public access and permission to use a dataset do not automatically establish that a third party may apply a new CC0 dedication to a redistributed copy. Therefore:

1. the GitHub portal may show a lightweight visualization derivative with full attribution;
2. the repository documents how users can obtain ANADEM from its official source;
3. the full original ANADEM subset remains outside the public Dryad upload until the applicable dataset license or written redistribution permission is recorded;
4. derived HAND and terrain products retain explicit ANADEM provenance and must be reviewed for compatibility with the repository selected for deposition.

This separation protects reproducibility without silently relicensing the authors' source dataset.
